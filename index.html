Projects

Global Supplier Contract Standardization Roadmap

During my Global Supply Management internship, I developed a strategic roadmap to support global supplier contract standardization.

Project Scope

  • Reviewed 40+ supplier contract templates
  • Analyzed data across 116 countries and ~10k suppliers
  • Drove and initiated coordination with legal and sourcing partners in all regions globally

My Approach

Establish what contracts the scope of the project was in and which clause I would seek to standardize. Find out how regions are divided globally, sub regions, and the countries that fall under through extensive research. From my findings and emails sent, seek out correct legal/sourcing contact responsible for updating each MSA, MSupA, and PO terms/conditions contract in each region Medtronic operates in. Initiate the proposed change through North America template update to correct legal/sourcing contact. Led meetings with legal partners in LATAM, Greater China, Southeast Asia, and Korea.

Outcome

Strategically created roadmap based on challenges/limitations, inconsistencies, legal/sourcing contact, pushback, areas of support, data by region, sub region, country, and supplier.

Produced a quarter-by-quarter implementation roadmap and centralized source of information for future decision-making. This work allows for my manager to execute on the remaining portion of the project. There is now easily accessible up to date data. This work enables the company to keep track on their near term goals and create standardization to a global clause on the expected timeline.

Update to Near term KPI tracker 27K+ Suppliers

Throughout the internship I analyzed raw data to extract information needed for the Responsible Supply Management Team's short term goal.

Project Scope

  • Improved visibility into progress toward near-term supplier emissions-target goals
  • Identified and ranked top suppliers by CO2e emissions
  • Analyzed 4 raw datasets to consolidate supplier sustainability information

My Approach

Performed data integration across 4 raw datasets, cross-referencing and leveraging XLOOKUPs, to map 27K+ supplier data to identify top suppliers by CO2e emissions, vertical category, subcategory, category manager, and % of total supplier emissions.

Outcome

This allowed the Responsible Supply Management Team to have visibility in the company's near term goal to have X% of suppliers setting emissions targets by X date. This is needed in order to keep on schedule for long term initiatives. In parallel this work allows for Category Management/Supplier Relationship Management to see high risk suppliers with updated data for emissions.
